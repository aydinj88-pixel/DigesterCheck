Covered plug-flow / DVO-type

Long, covered digester configurations commonly used in agricultural applications. Feedstock characteristics, bedding and grit can influence where material settles along the flow path.

BIOGAS

Covered anaerobic lagoons

Large-volume covered basins can accumulate settled material over time. Available depth, solids distribution and operating history are important context when considering usable capacity.

CSTR / mixed tanks

Continuously mixed tank systems rely on effective mixing and usable volume. Changes in solids behavior, mixing or pumping can be useful indicators for further investigation.

Configuration illustrations are educational and are not OEM drawings. “DVO-type” describes a general covered plug-flow style and does not imply affiliation with or authorization by any manufacturer.

HRT & usable volume

Why retention time matters.

Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT) is the average time material remains in the digester. In simplified terms:

Usable digester volume÷Daily influent flow=Approx. HRT

If settled material occupies part of the intended working volume, the effective usable volume—and therefore the effective retention time—may be lower than the original design basis. HRT alone does not prove a cleanout is required, but it is one useful piece of the assessment.
