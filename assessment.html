Our Assessment

Simple information.
Better decisions.

A focused review designed to help determine whether further investigation or cleanout planning should be considered.

What we review

Asset history

Digester type, age, dimensions, available drawings, commissioning information and previous cleanout history.

Feedstock

Manure, bedding, food waste, SSO and other inputs that can influence accumulation.

Operations

Available operating history and reported changes in mixing, pumping, throughput and gas production.

Observed indicators

Known or reported evidence of sand, grit, sediment, solids or contaminants.

Desktop screening

We begin with information you already have. The objective is to identify whether available evidence supports continued monitoring, further investigation or cleanout planning.

Optional site assessment

Where useful, a site visit can provide additional operational context and allow discussions with facility personnel. DigesterCheck does not perform confined-space entry or physical cleanout work.

Our boundary is intentional. DigesterCheck does not provide structural certification, engineering certification, confined-space assessment, equipment certification or cleanout services. Specialized engineering, inspection or measurement may be recommended where appropriate.
