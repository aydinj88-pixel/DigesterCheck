Independent by design

Assessment before action.

DigesterCheck was built around a simple idea: before an owner commits to a major digester cleanout, they should have a clearer understanding of why the cleanout may—or may not—be necessary. We don’t sell cleaning equipment or perform cleanouts. Our role is assessment and information.
