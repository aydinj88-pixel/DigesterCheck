A focused second opinion

We don't clean digesters.
We help owners determine whether they should consider cleaning them.

DigesterCheck reviews operating history, feedstock, tank information and operational indicators associated with sand, grit, sediment and solids accumulation. Our role is to give owners a clearer basis for their next decision—not to sell the cleanout.
